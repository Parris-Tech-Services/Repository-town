# Paul Roe remembrance houses — Repository Town

**Public memorial house:** K3 (`Paul-Roe`).

**Quiet reflection house:** C2 (`Paul-Roe-Private-Archive`). The name refers
to a separate *private* archive; **nothing from that archive is published
by the town**.

The rooms are **symbolic memorial galleries**, not a reconstruction of Paul's
home, possessions, family conversations or private research. They are intended
to honour Paul Roe as a historian, teacher, storyteller, Christian and a
co-founder of Cornerstone Christian Community.

## Authored rooms

The K3 memorial has six spaces:

1. **Remembering Paul Roe:** quiet seating, flowers and public Order of Service.
2. **Bourke and Outback stories:** maps, writings and the places whose people Paul listened to.
3. **Voices and oral history:** 2WEB oral histories and links to preserved recordings.
4. **Cornerstone and faith:** the community and faith that shaped Paul's teaching.
5. **Books and the storyteller:** *Tell Me Another*, *Bourke and Beyond*, and Paul's public work.
6. **Shared memories:** family-approved public memorial and invitation to continue telling people's stories.

C2 provides only a **Quiet remembrance** area and a **Stories Paul shared**
area, without exposing private research, correspondence, health details,
family information or archive inventory.

The game does **not** simulate Paul Roe as an NPC or use invented speech in
his name. No dice, combat encounters, goblins, lucky dips, crystals, random
fortunes, fake private research access, or fabricated heirlooms appear in
these spaces.

## Room labels and navigation

The room title is centred within the physical bounds of its room. Every
generated room and exhibit label records the **room index** it belongs to,
and the 3D renderer hides it when the visitor is in a different room. This
prevents a bathroom sign from appearing to designate a living room.

The two memorial buildings no longer inherit a stranger's autogenerated
household rooms, bathroom, laundry, bedroom or generic upper floors.

## Implementation and acceptance

- Runtime overlay: `world/paul-roe-tribute.js`, loaded by `main.js`.
  Overlays survive future regeneration of generic `houses.json`; other
  houses are returned unchanged.
- Memorial-aware room builder: `world/interior.js`.
- Tests: `node tests/paul-roe-memorial.mjs`.
- CI: `.github/workflows/paul-roe-memorial.yml`.

Any future photograph, specific memory, personal anecdote, audio clip or
family-submitted remembrance should come from approved public material
with the relevant submitter's permission. Never insert sensitive archive
contents or invent memories to fill a room.
